# opencode.cafe — заявка в каталог

Куда: https://opencode.cafe — комьюнити-каталог расширений opencode.
На сайте есть механизм «Share your own creations» (кнопка Submit / форма) —
найти на главной и заполнить полями ниже.

---

**Name:** Idea Inbox

**Type:** Plugin

**Tags:** `#backlog` `#tui` `#productivity`

**Description:**

> A sidebar backlog for stray thoughts. Capture a thought with leader+z
> without derailing your agent, then dispatch it as a mission with leader+i
> and watch it move from parked to running to done. Agents manage the backlog
> via tools.

**Link:** https://github.com/apilot/opencode-idea-inbox
